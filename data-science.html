Cornerstone Project: Weeks 1 & 2

NIPS 2010 Validation 2025-11

Understanding & Validating "A Dirty Model for Multi-task Learning"

Conducted an empirical validation and mathematical replication of the seminal research by Jalali et al. (NIPS 2010). Formulated a convex composite estimator that decomposes high-dimensional parameter matrices into a superposition of shared block-sparse components ($\ell_1/\ell_\infty$) and task-specific element-wise sparse components ($\ell_1$). Solved via CVXPY and validated the theoretical sample complexity threshold $(2-\alpha)s\log p$ through Monte Carlo phase transition sweeps.

Core Problem Formulation:
$$\Theta = B + S \quad \text{with} \quad (\hat{S}, \hat{B}) = \arg\min_{S, B} \frac{1}{2n}\sum_{k=1}^r \|y^{(k)} - X^{(k)}(S^{(k)}+B^{(k)})\|_2^2 + \lambda_s \|S\|_{1,1} + \lambda_b \|B\|_{1,\infty}$$
✓ Proved sample complexity $(2-\alpha)s\log p$ beats independent Lasso ✓ Replicated sharp phase transition at $\theta \approx 1.0$ across 3 overlap regimes ✓ Implemented with CVXPY and log-scale grid search hyperparameter tuning
